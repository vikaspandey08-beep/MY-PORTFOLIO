About me

A developer who started with the hard part first

I'm Vikas Gyanendrakumar Pandey, a developer from Nagpur who found his footing in Java. I completed my BCA at Shri Mathuradas Mohota College of Science under RTMNU, and I'm continuing with an MCA at G. H. Raisoni College of Engineering and Management.

I began with console applications — an ATM simulator, a student record system, a currency converter. No interface to hide behind, so every wrong input, every invalid balance and every edge case had to be solved in the logic itself. That habit stayed with me.

Today I work on both halves of the same problem: Java, Spring Boot and MySQL on the server, React, JavaScript and CSS in the browser, with OOP, DBMS and operating systems as the foundation underneath. The goal is simple — build full stack Java web applications that people actually use, and get sharper at it every semester.
